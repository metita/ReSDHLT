ReSDHLT 0.21.0 makes RAD faster without changing the light it writes: on the
CPU the `.bsp` is byte for byte the one 0.20.0 writes.

## Highlights

- RAD: samples gather light in blocks of 8x8 lightmap-cache points. A block
  drops, once, every light that each of its samples would have dropped one by
  one: behind the light's plane or the surface, outside a spot cone, or under
  `-lightskip`. On fp_squidgame_thno the light loop went from 1,469 million
  iterations to 190 million.
- RAD: shadow rays are proven in bundles. After the first ray of a block toward
  a light, the whole bundle from the block to the light is walked down the BSP;
  when it is proven all clear or all blocked, no other sample of the block
  traces that ray. On fp_squidgame_thno 25 million of 168 million rays are
  still traced, on ze_sanctorum 34 of 99 million. BuildVisLeafs does the same
  between groups of patches.
- RAD: with `-vismatrix auto` and the GPU (the GUI default), maps under 32,768
  patches keep the transfers on the CPU, where they are faster. The result with
  the GPU is now the CPU's to within a few bytes of 1/255.

Whole RAD, GUI arguments, against 0.20.0: fp_squidgame_thno 1.39x on the CPU
and 1.30x with the GPU, ze_sanctorum 1.21x, guard 1.56x with the GPU; maps with
few lights and much sky, like de_inferno, gain little. Checked on eleven maps,
AVX2 and portable builds; measurements in `docs/BENCHMARKS.md` section 11.
