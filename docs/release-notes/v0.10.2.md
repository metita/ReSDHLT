ReSDHLT 0.10.2 fixes a leak that 0.10.1 introduced and closes the seams left by
non-planar vertex-manipulated brushes.

## Highlights

- Fixed: maps that compiled with 0.10.0 could leak with 0.10.1, so they got no
  portal file and no VIS. Slits between brushes meant to touch (for example one
  side at 2079.98 and the other at 2080) are closed again for the leak check.
- Fixed: brushes with non-planar faces left by vertex manipulation in J.A.C.K.
  or Hammer compile as the solid the editor shows, so no more see-through seams
  between neighbouring pieces. Brushes whose faces are planar are untouched;
  `-noconvexfix` in the CSG options turns this off.
- `scripts/holecheck.py` no longer reports faces that the engine does draw, and
  also checks `func_detail`.
