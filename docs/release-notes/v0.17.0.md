ReSDHLT 0.17.0 fixes dark moving entities under ambient occlusion and trims
the shadow rays RAD traces for nothing.

## Highlights

- Fixed: with `-ao` or `-aoall`, a func_plat, func_door, func_train or any
  other entity that moves came out dark. Its occlusion was baked where it
  starts (a plat down in its shaft, walled in) and went with it everywhere.
  Those entities get no ambient occlusion now. `zhlt_noao 1` turns it off on
  any other brush entity.
- New: `-lightskip` (default 0.001). A light that could add less than a
  thousandth of a lightmap step to a sample gets no shadow ray, on the CPU and
  in the GPU kernel. On ze_elysium_b1 that is 19% of all shadow rays: 33.7 s to
  31.8 s with the GPU, 70.9 s to 65.1 s on the CPU. 1% of the lighting bytes
  move by 1/255. `-lightskip 0` gives the previous output byte for byte.
