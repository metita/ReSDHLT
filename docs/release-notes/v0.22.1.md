ReSDHLT 0.22.1 fixes `zhlt_embedlightmap` on water. Every other surface compiles
to the same `.bsp` as in 0.22.0.

## Fixed

- RAD: embedded lightmaps on water no longer show a dark grid (#6). The engine
  maps water textures its own way (no texture shift, one repeat every 64 units,
  an 8 unit wave warp), and the bake assumed the mapping of ordinary faces.
  Water is now baked for the engine's mapping, so the light carries on across
  face edges and the water texture keeps its in-game scale.
- BSP: the water of `zhlt_embedlightmap` entities is cut into 80 unit pieces so
  each fits its baked texture. Embedded water warps twice as far as plain
  water.
