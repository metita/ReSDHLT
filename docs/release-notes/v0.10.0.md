ReSDHLT 0.10.0 makes VIS output reproducible and speeds up CSG and VIS without
changing what they produce.

## Highlights

- Fixed: VIS `-full` wrote a different `.bsp` depending on thread timing. Every
  thread count now writes the same `.bsp` as `-threads 1`, on Windows and Linux.
- VIS: fixed flow order plus a scheduler where a waiting thread helps finish
  the portal it waits for. Near-linear scaling up to the physical core count.
- VIS: about 20% less work per flow (separator candidates decided without
  normalizing when certain, branchless `ChopWinding`, 64-bit bitsets).
- CSG: indexed plane lookups and `CSGBrush` on all threads with output written
  in brush order. About 2x faster on the maps measured.
- Every change was verified byte-identical against the previous single-threaded
  output on 13 real CS 1.6 maps.

The measurements, including what was tried and dropped, are in
[docs/BENCHMARKS.md](https://github.com/metita/ReSDHLT/blob/main/docs/BENCHMARKS.md) section 8.
