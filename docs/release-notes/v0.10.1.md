ReSDHLT 0.10.1 fixes faces that disappear in game after vertex manipulation.

## Highlights

- Fixed: a brush face shaped with vertex manipulation could be see-through in
  game while J.A.C.K. showed a closed solid, usually only when the brush touched
  others. Neighbouring faces left almost coplanar by off-grid points confused
  sdHLBSP in three places; all three are fixed. On 32 generated
  vertex-manipulated maps the see-through spots went from 469 to 28, the rest
  being sub-unit cracks in pieces deformed by less than 2 units.
- Maps without that geometry are not affected: the real CS 1.6 maps checked
  compile to the same geometry as with 0.10.0.
- New `scripts/holecheck.py` ray-casts a compiled `.bsp` and reports where the
  player would see through a wall:
  `python3 scripts/holecheck.py yourmap.bsp --map yourmap.map`
